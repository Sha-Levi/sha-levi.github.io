Where the money goes

CONTEXTVERIFIED

The State holds the revenue first and may keep up to one percent for collecting it.

What the voter is told
Capital Project Sales Tax dollars raised in Chesterfield County are not shared with the State. pennywithapurpose_home.txt

The Act: The revenues of the tax collected under this article must be remitted to the Department of Revenue and placed on deposit with the State Treasurer, less costs to the Department of Revenue of administering the tax, not to exceed one percent of the revenues. sc_code_4-10_art3_clean.txt

Then the State Treasurer shall distribute the revenues quarterly to the county treasurer, and the county treasurer or the county administrator shall certify to the Department of Revenue amounts of net proceeds applied to the costs of each project. sc_code_4-10_art3_clean.txt

Section two

“Penny tax or property tax”

How the choice is framed to the voter, and what the ballot itself says about property tax.

Two paths

CONTEXTVERIFIED

A yes vote also carries property tax: the existing millage, and the ad valorem backstop.

What the voter is told
Voters will decide how it gets funded — through a temporary penny sales tax, or through property taxes. pennywithapurpose_home.txt
The record says

The ballot itself, on a yes vote: provided further that in the event the sales and use tax to be imposed as stated herein is inadequate for the payment of such bonds, such bonds shall be payable from an ad valorem tax imposed on all taxable property in Chesterfield County. ballot_question.txt

The county's own funding plan for a yes vote: the project would be funded through the temporary 1% Capital Project Sales Tax together with the county's existing debt service millage, with no increase in property taxes. pennywithapurpose_home.txt

The commission's cost, a project with a total estimated cost of approximately $50,000,000, against its revenue projection, $44,000,000 during the eight (8) year period, leaves 6 million dollars to come from another source. res_2026-02.txt

The sign above carries no sponsor's name. The state's disclosure rule, SECTION 8-13-1354. Identification of person independently paying for election-related communication; exemptions., reaches communications supporting or opposing a public official, a candidate, or a ballot measure and then exempts them: Campaign buttons, balloons, yard signs, or similar items are exempt from this requirement. The exemption dates from 1995 Act No. 6, SECTION 43. sc_code_t08c013.txt

If the question fails

CONTEXTVERIFIED

The millage path is the county’s stated estimate. It is not on the ballot.

What the voter is told
If the Penny Sales Tax is not approved, the county would need to rely on property taxes to finance the project. County officials estimate that the debt service millage would increase from 7 mills to at least 20 mills for approximately 30 years to repay the cost of construction. pennywithapurpose_home.txt
The record says

From 7 to at least 20 mills is an increase of at least 13 mills, by the county's own estimate. pennywithapurpose_home.txt

That alternative was put to Council before the question was set. The briefing minutes of MAY 18, 2026: Mrs. Stanley presented Council with two (2) options for funding the prospective Chesterfield County Detention Center. The second was funding it with a Property Tax Millage Increase. cc_council_briefing_minutes_2026-05-18.txt

The ballot question does not mention millage or a property tax increase if the question fails. ballot_question.txtsearched: the files tagged; terms: mill · increase

What governs a foundation

CONTEXTVERIFIED

Whether a sheriff’s foundation is a public body depends on whether it takes or spends public money.

What the voter is told
Exploring the creation of a nonprofit foundation to provide community-supported funding for critical needs beyond the county budget. commitment-moving-forward.txt
The record says

The charitable-solicitation statute: This chapter may be cited as the "South Carolina Solicitation of Charitable Funds Act". What it requires: a charitable organization which intends to solicit contributions within this State or have contributions solicited on its behalf must file a registration statement with the Secretary of State. sc_code_t33c056.txt

The chapter does not mention governance, bylaws, conflicts of interest, public funds or a public body. sc_code_t33c056.txtsearched: the files tagged; terms: governance · bylaws · conflict of interest · public funds · public body

The Freedom of Information Act's definition of a public body reaches further: any organization, corporation, or agency supported in whole or in part by public funds or expending public funds. sc_code_t30c004.txt

Section eight

Your voice

The author’s statement, a link to the full county record, and a request any voter can send.

The author’s statement — not part of the record

You are not choosing between a penny tax and a property tax. You are being asked one question: do you authorize $50,000,000 in bonds, funded by a one-cent sales tax, for a new detention center, operated by this sheriff’s office, overseen by this county government, with this financial record.

A yes vote is consent. A no vote is a question: show us you can manage what you have before we give you more.

The county budget record and the records-request templates for the jail inspection, the Sheriff’s Office and the county’s finances are on the earlier page, Extraction With No End.
