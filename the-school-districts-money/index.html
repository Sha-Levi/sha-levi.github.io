Budget

Budget up sixty-two percent; instruction’s share down, then up in the newest budget

The approved General Fund budget over fourteen years, and how much of each recent budget goes to instruction.

The district’s approved General Fund budget grew from forty-eight point six million dollars to seventy-eight point eight million over fourteen years — an increase of sixty-two percent. Over the same span, the district’s own self-reported administrative cost grew two hundred twenty percent. And instruction’s share of the budget — the portion that goes to teaching — fell. In one budget year, the amount budgeted for instruction dropped by seven hundred seventy-nine thousand, eight hundred four dollars while the total budget grew by two point four million dollars. The money went somewhere. In that year the increase did not go to instruction. In the newest budget, instruction’s share rose again.

FY2012-13 General Fund budget (budget series): $48,620,679.81. FY2025-26 General Fund budget (budget series): $78,802,776.00. An increase of 62.1 percent. ccsd_tallies.txtccsd_budget_growth.csv

Board of Education

Board salaries budgeted at forty-two thousand, then six hundred twenty-eight thousand twice, then eighty-eight thousand

The Board of Education function in four consecutive approved budgets.

The first year at six hundred twenty-eight thousand dollars carried no employee benefits; the second carried eighteen thousand dollars.
